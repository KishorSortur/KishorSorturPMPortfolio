Why a technical PM

The instinct transfers.

For three years my job was to take a vague complaint — something's slow, something failed overnight — and trace it to a cause I could prove. Embedded validation frameworks, ML pipelines for predictive maintenance, production incidents at enterprise scale. The habit that built was refusing to accept the first plausible explanation.

That's the same habit product management runs on. My capstone research started with a finding I had to throw out: the first draft split the problem two ways, and the interviews showed it was one barrier surfacing differently. Catching that is the job.

Confident Alternatives

ixigo product capstone · academic case study · 2026

The problem

Only 26% of ixigo Trains users resolve a booking within 24 hours after their preferred train comes back unavailable. Target: 42%.

What research actually showed

20 research interactions — survey plus in-depth interview. My first read split this into two separate problems. It wasn't. It was one barrier — users can't quickly reach a booking they trust will confirm — showing up in two forms: confirmation-path failure (~40%) and manual comparison fatigue (~60%).

For me the problem is confirmation of tickets on my desired date and class — not how to find alternatives.Research interview
Frustrating when I have to open several trains and compare them one by one, especially when seats are getting booked while I'm checking.Survey respondent

What I designed

15 candidate solutions scored with RICE. Two carried into the prototype, each aimed at one manifestation of the barrier.

Resurface Existing AlternativesRICE 113.3

Surfaces ixigo's own underused alternate-booking tools the moment a train goes unavailable — ranked, labelled High/Medium/Low rather than a false-precision percentage, with a visible commitment that ranking reflects fit, not partner priority.

Confirmation-Odds ExplainerRICE 80.0

An optional, lightweight explainer for what a confidence label means — addressing the trust gap without forcing a tutorial on anyone.

Projected impact

Today 26%Target 42%

Booking resolution within 24 hours. Modelled across all three affected travel modes: ~₹38.8 crore estimated incremental annual revenue, based on capstone assumptions. Full metrics system defined — North Star, leading and lagging indicators, and three guardrails.
