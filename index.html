Capstone project — completed 2026

Confident Alternatives — ixigo Product Capstone

A complete 9-step PM capstone (academic case study): research, problem framing, solution design, prototyping, GTM, and metrics — for helping train travellers resolve an unavailable booking with confidence.

The challenge

ixigo Trains users who hit an unavailable preferred train only resolve the booking 26% of the time within 24 hours. Target: 42%.

Research

20 survey responses + 1 in-depth interview showed this wasn't one problem — it was a single barrier (inability to quickly and confidently reach a confirmed booking) surfacing two ways: ~40% hit confirmation-path failure, ~60% hit manual comparison fatigue.

"For me the problem is confirmation of tickets on my desired date and class — not how to find alternatives."
— research interview
"Frustrating when I have to open several trains and compare them one by one, especially when seats are getting booked while I'm checking."
— survey respondent

The solution

Resurface Existing AlternativesRICE 113.3

Automatically surfaces ixigo's own underused alternate-booking tools the moment a preferred train is unavailable — ranked, confidence-labeled (High/Medium/Low, never a raw percentage), with a visible "ranked by fit for you, not by partner" fairness commitment.

Confirmation-Odds ExplainerRICE 80.0

A lightweight, optional explainer addressing the trust barrier directly — why a confidence label means what it means, without a forced tutorial.
